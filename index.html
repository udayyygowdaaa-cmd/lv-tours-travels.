FAQ

Questions we often hear

How do I book a package?

Fill in the booking form above and send it on WhatsApp or email. We confirm availability and share the itinerary and price before you pay anything.

Can packages be customised?

Yes. Every package can be changed for your dates, hotel category, budget and group size.

What is the payment process?

A small advance confirms your booking and the balance is paid before departure. Payment details are shared after you approve the itinerary.

Do you handle visas for international trips?

We guide you on documents and help with the application process. Final approval is always decided by the embassy.
